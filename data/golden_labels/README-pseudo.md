# Pseudo-gold mining (P0-1) — provenance and rules

Expands the golden set beyond the 5 self-frozen offset-gold filings via
multi-teacher voting. Spec: `docs/research/giants_task2.md` §2 P0-1 and §2.1.
Miner: `tools/mine_pseudo_gold.py`. Form-string census evidence:
`tools/count_forms.py`.

## Teachers and lineage

| teacher | lineage | role |
|---|---|---|
| our pipeline | this repo | produces the offset-exact span under vote |
| edgartools (pinned 5.42.0, MIT) | independent | engine vote via `compare_item(source="edgartools")`; its items 10–16 blind class (FG-SEC-006) yields `engine_suspect` = no-signal, never a strike (P0-5) |
| EDGAR-CORPUS (HF `eloukas/edgar-corpus`, Apache-2.0) | **edgar-crawler** | weak machine labels — a vote, **never gold on its own**; table-stripped (`remove_tables=True` at build), so compared via `compare_item(source="corpus")` containment only |

**Lineage discount**: EDGAR-CORPUS labels are generated by edgar-crawler
(build config pinned at crawler commit `058a121a41`). If an edgar-crawler
engine vote is ever added (P0-7a), corpus + crawler agreement counts as ONE
independent confirmation, not two.

## Vote tiers

- `pseudo_gold_3way` — ours + edgartools + corpus agree → freezable candidate
  (two independent teacher lineages).
- `pseudo_gold_2way_corpus_only` — corpus agrees, engine unavailable/suspect
  (typical pre-2001 degradation, recorded honestly) → weak candidate; do NOT
  freeze without human spot-check.
- `hard_case` — any teacher disagreement → `hard_case_queue.json`, human
  adjudication only.
- `no_corpus_signal` — corpus column carries no signal under the era schema.

## Guardrails (§2.1)

- **g1** join collisions drop /A and the KSB family first.
- **g2** PRE2003 corpus `section_14` = canonical 15 (Exhibits); an empty
  `section_15` is no-signal, never an accountant-fees teacher vote.
- **g3** 2016–2020 corpus `section_15` tails carry Item 16 pollution — cut at
  "Item 16" before comparing.
- **g4** KSB filings degrade to 2-way (the corpus never holds KSB); the
  `ksb_to_canonical` shift mapping (6=MD&A → 7, …, 13 → 15; canonical 6/7A
  NOT_APPLICABLE) applies on the direct-extraction side.
- **g5** /A amendments never enter pseudo-gold (Rule 12b-15 partial
  restatements) — hard-case queue.
- **g6** the corpus filename is `{cik}_{year}` with no accession; the join
  reverse-looks-up the accession from (CIK, year) via the submissions API.
- **g7** /A volume is ~17–25% of 10-K volume — never assumed rare.

Form normalization is a FULL enumeration (`10KSB`/`10KSB40`/`10KT405` have no
hyphen; `startswith("10-K")` misses the KSB family, `== "10-K"` misses every
variant). Era schema: Release 33-8183 (FYE ≥ 2003-12-15 → 14=Fees, 15=Exhibits),
double-checked against the filing's own Item 14 heading in boundary years.

## Artifacts (downloaded/derived data — never committed)

- `data/sec_eval/pseudo_gold/pseudo_gold_candidates.json` — per-filing,
  per-item verdicts, tiers and offsets (`start_offset`/`end_offset`/
  `text_sha256` addressing `NormalizedDocument.text`).
- `data/sec_eval/pseudo_gold/hard_case_queue.json` — join failures, /A cases,
  our-side unsupported eras, teacher splits.
- `data/sec_eval/pseudo_gold/corpus_cache/` — streamed corpus records
  (bounded: first K per era-stratified year, ≤ 30 filings per run).

Freezing into `data/golden_labels/offsets/` stays with the existing
`tools/freeze_offset_gold.py` protocol (human spot-check before commit);
`pseudo_gold_2way_corpus_only` items must additionally pass the κ-gated human
protocol (P1-4) before they may be frozen.

**Calibration de-correlation (P0-3)**: edgartools is both a teacher here and a
confidence-signal source in triangulation. When pseudo-gold feeds calibration,
either exclude the edgartools vote from the gold definition or report the
pseudo-gold curve as auxiliary only — otherwise correlated errors inflate AUROC.
