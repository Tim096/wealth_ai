fix(sec): kill three silent-failure classes found by 11-company audit

An adversarial audit (56 agents over 11 real 10-Ks) confirmed 31 anomalies
that the 3-ticker smoke test missed. Three systemic classes, now fixed in
a new refine.py:

1. reference-stub mislabeling (biggest class): the old _CROSS_REF_RE only
   caught "refer to Item N". Real filings point elsewhere with many
   phrasings the regex missed - "Refer to Note 14", "Reference is made to
   the Financial Section", "appear on pages 162-314", proxy incorporation
   with words between "incorporated" and "by reference". classify_reference_
   stub now detects short pointer bodies broadly and marks them
   incorporated_by_reference with the target named. Fixes MSFT/NVDA/CAT
   Item 3, JPM 1C/7/7A/8, GS 1C/7A/11/13/14, XOM 3/7/7A/8, NVDA 8.

2. trailing furniture leak: every part-boundary item (4, 9C, 16) leaked
   "PART II/III", page numbers, and running headers into its span because
   end = next item start. trim_trailing_furniture strips them explainably.

3. terminal runaway (most severe): XOM Item 16 swallowed 311K chars and
   JPM Item 15 swallowed 985K chars of an appended Financial Section /
   bound annual report. detect_appended_section_cut caps the terminal item
   at the section break and warns that appended content was excluded.

Confidence now discriminates: a new content_substantiveness component
(and zeroing verifier credit for stubs) drops stub confidence to ~0.57-0.7
vs ~0.95 for substantive items - the audit found a flat 0.958 regardless.

Net effect on the 11-company sweep: pass 75.9%->70.0%, incorporated_by_
reference 19.0%->24.9%. Lower pass rate = higher honesty; 15 silent-failure
stubs reclassified. 42 tests pass (10 new in test_refine.py).
